Lantern Tour
Oct. 15 at 6:00pm

Join us for an evening of Spooky Lore around the Thiel College campus. Enjoy stories of old and things that go bump in the night while also learning about all Thiel has to offer through the orange glow of campus lights.

This event will feature a next steps meeting with an Admissions Counselor to explain more about life as a Tomcat, available scholarships and more. After your tour and meeting, you and your guests can mingle with fellow Tomcats around the fire with complimentary drinks and s'mores.

Agenda:
6:00pm Check-in to James Pedas Center at the Admissions Desk
6:15pm Learn More about Thiel College and your next steps
6:30pm Campus Tour featuring legends from the past throughout campus
7:30pm End Tour at the campfire for drinks & s'mores


All senior students that attend this visit day are eligible to obtain a $500 visit grant to use toward their Fall 2027 Thiel account.
